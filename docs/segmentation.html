Strategic Maritime Trade Corridor Ranking

This analysis ranks bilateral maritime cereal corridors using a precomputed opportunity score combining trade volume, transport cost conditions, and liner shipping connectivity. The ranking is intended to identify strategically significant maritime trade lanes within the 2021 cereal trade network.

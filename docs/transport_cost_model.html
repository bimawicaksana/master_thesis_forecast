Maritime Transport Cost Modelling

Gravity-based econometric and machine-learning models for analysing and predicting bilateral maritime transport costs for cereal commodities.

MVP

Pooled OLS and Gamma GLM provide global structural benchmarks; fixed-effect models capture commodity and country-level heterogeneity. Ridge provides a regularized predictive benchmark. ANN captures nonlinear relationships and was retained in the thesis forecasting framework. Evaluation samples and target scales differ across these model families.

Research methodology

Results are based on the defended master’s thesis. Model evaluation scopes and target scales differ across methods and should be interpreted accordingly.

The implemented target is maritime transport expenditure per ton; the log target is log maritime transport expenditure per ton.

Pooled Econometric Benchmarks

The pooled specifications estimate average structural relationships across the transport-cost sample. OLS models the log-transformed cost target, while the Gamma GLM models the positive level cost using a log link. Their error metrics therefore should not be interpreted as directly comparable on a common numerical scale.

Commodity Fixed-Effect Models

Fixed-effect specifications capture structural heterogeneity across commodities and trading countries. Model fit is evaluated within each commodity-specific estimation sample.

R² uses log maritime transport expenditure per ton. RMSE, MAPE, bias and extreme errors use level expenditure per ton. Bias = predicted − actual. Extreme error threshold: relative level error > 2.0.

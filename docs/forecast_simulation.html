Scenario analysis

The simulations examine first-order responses of maritime cereal trade and transport costs to geopolitical, climatic and regulatory shocks.

Conditional scenario analysis

Scenario results show conditional changes relative to their respective baseline assumptions. They are analytical stress tests rather than probabilistic forecasts of future outcomes.

Model / Scenario Interpretation

Black Sea Conflict

Conditional geopolitical, supply and maritime shock affecting UKR/RUS, with a wheat/maize focus and approximately 85% of eligible baseline volume selected.

El Niño

Climate / production-led case for ARG/AUS. This scenario includes transport shocks as specified in the thesis results table; methodology text also describes logistics as broadly intact.

EU Emissions Trading System

Policy / maritime-cost case on EU-linked corridors with a +12% freight shock. Intended, configured and implemented product scopes differ.

Methodological notes
  1. Scenario results are conditional stress tests, not probabilistic predictions.
  2. Each scenario uses its own baseline. Absolute baseline values reported in the thesis are shown separately and do not underpin these scenario indices.
  3. Annual and product totals aggregate the forecast panel as supplied, including repeated records and records with incomplete country identifiers. They are panel summaries, not a deduplicated census of world trade.
  4. Corridor views use complete, unique year/origin/destination/product identities. Exclusion from pairing does not imply those records must be excluded from every aggregate. Some country-code/label mappings require review; interpret country labels alongside their ISO codes.
  5. The product aggregate-volume metric differs from the historical arithmetic mean of observation-level indices.
  6. The cost index is scoped and unweighted, with a fixed product scope. It is not a global or trade-weighted index.
  7. EU ETS intended wheat/maize/rice; selection used wheat/maize; the transport shock applies to all eight cereal products on the selected corridors.
  8. El Niño follows the results-table assumptions including transport shocks, while methodology text describes logistics as broadly intact. This contradiction is not resolved here.
  9. Importer cost-burden analysis is deferred pending denominator and sample validation. No importer exposure, FOB burden or food-security burden score is presented.

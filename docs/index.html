Maritime Agri-Food Trade Forecast Dashboard

Interactive dashboards accompanying the Master Thesis:
“Segmentation and Forecasting of Maritime Agri-Food Trade for Food Security: A Gravity–Machine Learning Approach”
Author: Bima surya wicaksana

About the Research

This dashboard accompanies the submitted master’s thesis and provides an open-access view of the empirical results developed in the study. The research examines bilateral maritime trade flows of food-security-relevant agricultural commodities using a combined gravity–machine learning framework. Econometric specifications (OLS and PPML) are employed to identify structural trade relationships, while artificial neural networks are used to enhance short-horizon forecasting performance under heterogeneous and volatile trade conditions. The dashboard is designed to support transparency, replicability, and policy-relevant interpretation of the thesis results.

Dashboards

Trade & Risk Segmentation

Segmentation of countries, commodities, and bilateral corridors based on observed maritime agri-food trade patterns, dependency, and food-security relevance using historical data.

Forecast Transport Cost Model

Overview of maritime transport cost modelling results, comparing econometric gravity-based specifications and machine learning approaches, including model diagnostics and performance metrics.

Trade Flow Model

Bilateral maritime trade flow modelling results using structural gravity models and artificial neural networks, highlighting explanatory power and predictive performance.

Forecast Simulation

Scenario-based simulation dashboard allowing users to explore forecasted maritime trade and transport cost outcomes by adjusting key assumptions. Simulations use estimated transport-cost and trade-flow models.

Methodology Summary

Data: Consolidated bilateral panel dataset (2015–2021) combining UNCTAD maritime trade flows, FAO Food Balance Sheet indicators, CEPII gravity variables, and liner shipping connectivity metrics.

Methods: Structural gravity models estimated via OLS and PPML with fixed effects, benchmarked against machine-learning-based forecasting using artificial neural networks (ANN).

Scope: Commodity- and corridor-level analysis of maritime agri-food trade with forward-looking baseline projections to support food security risk assessment.

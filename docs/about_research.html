Research Context

This dashboard is a public research showcase developed as a by-product of the master's thesis Segmentation and Forecasting of Maritime Cereal Trade for Food Security: A Bilateral Gravity–Machine Learning Framework.

The research was conducted by Bima Surya Wicaksana within the Master International Trade & Logistics programme at KEDGE Business School, supervised by Prof. Pierre Cariou. It was presented and defended as part of the programme's degree requirements in 2026.

Purpose of This Dashboard

The dashboard provides an accessible, interactive presentation of selected research outputs: maritime trade segmentation, transport-cost modelling, trade-flow modelling and forward scenario analysis.

It is a research communication and demonstration tool, not publication of the full thesis, complete raw datasets or full modelling and algorithmic pipeline. Its figures, tables and interactive outputs are selected and processed research results.

Data Use & Attribution

Publicly accessible institutional datasets were cleaned, transformed, integrated and modelled for academic research purposes. Original source datasets remain subject to the terms of use, licensing conditions and attribution requirements of their respective providers.

This dashboard primarily presents derived indicators, aggregated research results and model outputs rather than redistributing the original source databases. Visitors requiring original source data should obtain them directly from the respective data providers.

Dashboard design, research interpretation and derived analytical outputs are presented by the author. Original institutional data remain subject to their respective providers' terms.

Research Approach

The research combines gravity-based econometric methods and machine-learning approaches to analyse maritime cereal trade, transport costs and future scenario exposure.

The analytical framework includes regression and fixed-effect models, PPML, regularized machine learning, artificial neural networks, clustering and scenario-based stress testing.

This dashboard presents selected results from that framework. Detailed model specifications, preprocessing procedures and methodological discussions remain part of the underlying thesis research.

Scenario Interpretation

Forward-looking results are analytical simulations based on stated assumptions. They represent conditional responses to geopolitical, climatic or regulatory shocks, not probabilistic forecasts of future events.

The simulations assess first-order exposure within the research framework and do not model all possible adaptive responses, such as supplier substitution, rerouting or policy intervention.

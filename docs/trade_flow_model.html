Maritime Trade Flow Modelling

Gravity-based econometric and machine-learning models for bilateral maritime cereal trade volumes.

MVP

OLS provides interpretable gravity relationships in log trade volume. PPML models trade volume in levels and handles zero/nonnegative outcomes. Fixed-effect specifications capture structural heterogeneity. ANN captures nonlinear interactions and was retained as the main predictive trade-flow model in the thesis.

Research methodology

Model evaluation scopes and metric scales differ across methods and should be interpreted accordingly. ANN performance is presented using the evaluation metrics reported in the thesis.

Gravity-Econometric Models

OLS

Log-transformed maritime trade volume target. An interpretable linear gravity benchmark.

PPML

Maritime trade volume in tonnes: a level outcome. Model fit is reported as McFadden pseudo-R², rather than ordinary R².

Pooled

Control structural heterogeneity across products.
